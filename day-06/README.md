## 2. Client Meeting

#### Meeting Overview:

| Field        | Details                                       |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Meeting type | Online introductory meeting with a new client |
| Platform     | Google Meet / Zoom / Microsoft Teams          |
| Duration     | 30 to 60 minutes                              |

## Meeting Agenda and Flow

| Time      | Topic                                        | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0-5 min   | **Welcome and introductions**                | Greet, introduce everyone, confirm the time available                                                                                                                                                    |
| 5-10 min  | **Agenda and purpose**                       | "Today we want to understand your business and discuss how we can help."                                                                                                                                 |
| 10-30 min | **Client's business and needs**              | Let the client explain. Listen and ask questions.                                                                                                                                                        |
| 30-40 min | **Our introduction and website demo**        | Briefly present who we are, then show the demo (see [Presenting a Website Demo](#presenting-a-website-demo)). Take questions as they come (see [Handling Client Questions](#handling-client-questions)). |
| 40-50 min | **Scope, timeline, and budget (high level)** | Discuss expectations, deadlines, and budget range                                                                                                                                                        |
| 50-55 min | **Summary and next steps**                   | Recap key points, agree on actions, owners, and dates                                                                                                                                                    |
| 55-60 min | **Close**                                    | Thank the client, confirm the follow-up                                                                                                                                                                  |
