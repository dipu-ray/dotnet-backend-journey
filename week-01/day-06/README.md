#### CRM Data Collection & Management Workflow:

- **Multi-Channel Source Mapping:** Tracks and aggregates customer touchpoints across website forms, social media platforms, live chat, inbound emails, and phone logs.
- **Automated Data Capture:** Utilizes webhooks, API integrations, and tracking pixels to automatically ingest prospect details (name, contact info, geography) into the CRM upon form submission.
- **Behavioral Activity Tracking:** Automatically logs digital interactions, including website pages viewed, email open/click rates, and shopping cart abandonment events.
- **Standardized Manual Logging:** Enforces mandatory fields and drop-down structures for sales reps to manually log offline interactions like phone calls and discovery meetings.
- **Data Enrichment & Verification:** Integrates with third-party tools to automatically validate email addresses and append public company insights (e.g., industry, company size, revenue).
- **Automated Data Segmentation:** Tags and categorizes incoming records into dynamic lists based on target personas, budget tiers, and buying intent.
- **Deduplication Engine:** Runs real-time background algorithms matching emails and phone numbers to merge incoming data into existing profiles instead of creating duplicates.
- **Compliance & Consent Logging:** Stores time-stamped user consent records alongside customer profiles to ensure strict alignment with GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy standards.

## 2. New Client Meeting on Google Meet

### Before the Meeting

- Know the **client's name, business, and project idea**.
- Read the **agenda** and any earlier notes or brief.
- Know **your role**: listen, take notes, answer technical questions if asked.
- Test your **internet, mic, camera, and headphones**.
- Join the Google Meet link **5 minutes early**, with a clean background and good lighting.
- Keep a **notebook or notes document** ready.

### During the Meeting

- **Mute** when not speaking, and keep the camera on if the client does.
- **Listen carefully.** Do not interrupt the client or the senior team members.
- **Take notes** on requirements, features, deadline, budget, and decisions.
- Understand the **client's problem**, not only the features they ask for.
- Use **simple language**. Avoid heavy technical terms.
- If you do not know something, say: "I will check and confirm."
- **Do not promise** time, cost, or features. Let the team lead decide.
- Note any **technical doubts** to discuss with the team afterward.

### Key Things to Understand

- What the client wants and **why** they need it
- Who the **users** will be
- Must-have features and nice-to-have features
- **Deadline** and **budget** range
- Who the **main contact and decision maker** is
- **Next steps** and who does what

### After the Meeting

- **Review your notes** while they are fresh.
- Join the **internal team discussion**: share your understanding and technical thoughts.
- Write down your **action items** and deadlines.
- Note **unclear points** to ask the client through the team lead.

### Quick Do's and Don'ts

| Do                              | Don't                                 |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Be on time and polite           | Join late or unprepared               |
| Listen and take notes           | Interrupt or multitask                |
| Ask the team lead when unsure   | Guess or make promises                |
| Keep client information private | Share client details outside the team |
